Sources & Legal Framework

1. Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)

Jurisdiction: United States federal law.

The CFAA, codified at 18 U.S.C. § 1030, addresses certain types of unauthorized computer access and related conduct. In this fictional case, investigators would need to examine the evidence before deciding whether any law was violated. A suspicious login alone does not prove a crime occurred.

Read the official U.S. Code, Section 1030

2. NIST Cybersecurity Incident Response Guidance

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) publishes guidance on preparing for, responding to, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents. This supports the case's emphasis on preserving evidence and responding systematically.

Read NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3

Ethical Principles

  • Protect confidential information.
  • Preserve evidence for a fair investigation.
  • Avoid accusations without sufficient evidence.
  • Limit access to authorized personnel.
