Case File: The Midnight Breach

Welcome, Investigator. You have been assigned a cybersecurity incident involving a fictional organization called Northstar Technologies.

INCIDENT ALERT — 12:47 AM

Northstar Technologies detected unusual activity involving an employee account. Several failed login attempts were followed by a successful login outside normal business hours. The security team is concerned that someone may have accessed information without authorization.

Your Mission

Examine three evidence records, interpret what each record reveals, identify what information is still missing, and recommend an appropriate response.

Remember: evidence does not automatically prove who committed an incident or why it happened. A responsible investigator distinguishes confirmed facts from assumptions.
